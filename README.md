## 📖 Featured Recipes

### 1️⃣ Chocolate Chip Cookies 🍪
**Description:** Chewy cookies with gooey chocolate chips and golden edges.  
**Prep Time:** 15 mins | **Cook Time:** 12 mins | **Servings:** 24  
**Ingredients:** Butter, Sugar, Eggs, Flour, Baking Soda, Salt, Chocolate Chips  
**Instructions:**  
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).  
2. Cream butter and sugars.  
3. Add eggs and vanilla.  
4. Mix in dry ingredients, fold in chocolate chips.  
5. Scoop and bake 10–12 mins.  

![Chocolate Chip Cookies](images/cookies.png)

### 2️⃣ Spaghetti Carbonara 🍝
**Description:** Classic Italian pasta with creamy egg sauce and pancetta.  
**Prep Time:** 10 mins | **Cook Time:** 15 mins | **Servings:** 2  
**Ingredients:** Spaghetti, Eggs, Parmesan, Pancetta, Black Pepper  
**Instructions:**  
1. Cook spaghetti.  
2. Fry pancetta.  
3. Mix eggs and Parmesan.  
4. Combine pasta, pancetta, and egg mixture.  
5. Serve immediately.  

![Spaghetti Carbonara](images/spaghetti.png)

### 3️⃣ Fresh Lemonade 🍋
**Description:** Refreshing summer drink with zesty lemon flavor.  
**Prep Time:** 5 mins | **Cook Time:** 0 mins | **Servings:** 4  
**Ingredients:** Lemons, Sugar, Water, Ice  
**Instructions:**  
1. Squeeze lemons.  
2. Mix juice with sugar and water.  
3. Add ice and stir.  
4. Serve chilled.  

![Lemonade](images/lemonade.png)

## 🛠 Features

- Clean, card-based layout for recipes  
- Quick access to ingredients and step-by-step instructions  
- Fully responsive for mobile and desktop  
- Easy to add new recipes  
- Minimal HTML, CSS, and JavaScript  

---

## 🌟 Benefits

- Organize recipes in a visually appealing format  
- Shareable and easy to use for personal or public projects  
- Makes cooking faster and easier with structured instructions  
- Great for learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript by customizing your own cards  

---

## 💻 Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Structure and content  
- **CSS3** – Styling and responsive layout  
- **JavaScript** – Optional interactivity  
- **GitHub Pages** – Hosting live demo  

---

## ⚙️ Customization

- Add a new recipe by duplicating an existing card in `index.html`  
- Update images in the `images/` folder and reference them in HTML  
- Modify styles in `style.css` to change layout, colors, or fonts  
- Optional: Add JavaScript for interactive features (e.g., search or filter recipes)
